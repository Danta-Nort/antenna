Privacy policy

Short version: this site collects nothing about you. It has no forms, trackers, analytics or cookies, and it loads nothing from other sites. The Antenna app sends no telemetry.

This site

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no cookies.
  • No external scripts, fonts or images — everything is served from antenna.contact.
  • Invite links: the part of an address after “#” stays in your browser. This site never receives it.
  • Download buttons send you on to the file on GitHub, where GitHub’s privacy statement applies.

The site runs on Cloudflare Workers. Like any web server, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver the page; we keep no request logs.

The Antenna app

Antenna has no accounts and no servers of ours that carry your messages: messages and files go end to end encrypted between the devices of the people talking, directly or through a relay that only forwards encrypted traffic. The app sends no telemetry and no crash reports.

Once a day the app asks updates.stillbyte.dev whether a newer signed release exists. The request carries nothing about you, your contacts or your messages; like any web request it reveals the computer’s IP address to the host. To turn it off, put off into ~/.config/antenna/update-url.

Closed-beta requests

Before the public release, this site had a form for the closed beta. The addresses people sent through it are used only to tell them that Antenna is now available, and then deleted. To have yours deleted now, write to us.

Your rights

Write to hello@antenna.contact to ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. We will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
