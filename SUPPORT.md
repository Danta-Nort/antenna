# Getting help

- **How do I…?** The [user guide](docs/USER-GUIDE.md) and the [step-by-step guides](docs/guide/README.md) cover
  installing, linking devices, connecting agents, files and updates.
- **Something does not work.** Every message Antenna shows is listed with what to do in
  [If something does not work](docs/USER-GUIDE.md#if-something-does-not-work); known problems and their fixes are in
  [TROUBLESHOOTING.md](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
- **A bug or a feature request.** [Open an issue](https://github.com/Danta-Nort/antenna/issues/new/choose) using its
  form. Include `antenna --version` and your system.
- **A security problem.** Never in a public issue: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else:** hello@antenna.contact.
